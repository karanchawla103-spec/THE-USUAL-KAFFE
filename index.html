Craft & Heritage

From Coffee Bean & Dough to Your Table

How we maintain authentic quality in every single cup and plate served in Milap Market.

Phase 01

Ethical Roasting & Bean Selection

We source fresh high-altitude shade-grown Arabica beans from Chikmagalur estates, medium-dark roasted to preserve natural sweet cocoa undertones.

Phase 02

The Traditional Hand-Beaten Whip

Instead of mechanical shortcuts, our baristas hand-beat coffee crystals with raw cane sugar until an ultra-dense, glossy pale-gold caramel cloud emerges.

Phase 03

High-Flame Woks & Stone Deck Ovens

Our momos are steam-cooked in bamboo baskets, while our farmhouse pizzas crisp up on stone hearths and chowmein is flashed on roaring iron woks.

Phase 04

Midnight Hospitality & Warm Serving

Every order is garnished and presented with care, whether you are enjoying it at our cafe table, roadside in your car, or at home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping into The Usual Kaffèe.

Unlike automatic espresso steam wands, authentic Indian hand-beaten coffee is manually whisked with sugar crystals until it forms a creamy, glossy butterscotch froth. When hot milk is folded into it, the whipped layer floats on top like a cloud, offering unmatched silkiness and nostalgic flavor.
We are open every day from 11:00 AM until 12:00 Midnight. We accept kitchen dine-in and pickup orders up until 11:45 PM. It is the perfect spot for late night drives and midnight coffee catchups in West Delhi!
Our verified average spend is between ₹1 and ₹200 per head. For example, a hot hand-beaten coffee (₹89) paired with freshly steamed momos (₹99) comes out to under ₹190, making high quality dining affordable for students and families alike.
